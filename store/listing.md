# AMO listing

## Name
Proton Mail When Ready

## Summary
Hide your Proton Mail inbox until you're ready for it, so you can write or search without getting pulled into unread mail.

## Description
Opening your email to send one message shouldn't mean getting pulled into twenty new ones.

Proton Mail When Ready hides your inbox by default and shows a single "Show Inbox" button instead. Compose, search, and other folders work as normal.

Hidden until you click "Show Inbox":
- The inbox message list
- The category tabs and their unread dots
- The Inbox unread count in the sidebar and tab title

Privacy: collects no data, makes no network requests, and only runs on mail.proton.me.

Not affiliated with Proton AG or the Inbox When Ready extension for Gmail.

Open source: https://github.com/taziksh/proton-mail-when-ready
